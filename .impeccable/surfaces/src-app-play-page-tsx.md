# Paper Plane Run

Mode: Experience. Audience: portfolio visitors who want a short playful break. Task: freely fly a paper plane through a rotating architectural course. Constraint: inherit the black-and-white, angular portfolio identity with no programming metaphor; use the existing Drei cloud implementation for the side atmosphere.

Direction: a fixed, low cinematic flight shot recreates the reference in 3D: a near-black sky and deep black, camera-following ocean surround a wet-black runway deck that floats 1.65 m above the water. Its thin white perimeter rails, center seam, and monumental beveled frames are self-lit architectural elements with restrained neon bloom; Drei's depth-sorted cloud banks form low, naturally shaped atmosphere outside the rails. The white paper plane stays near the lower center while rotating frames continuously cycle through the composition as atmosphere, never as scoring gates or obstacles. HUD is spare, high-contrast, and secondary to flight, with an explicit sound toggle beside Exit. A local CC0 synth loop begins only after Start Flight, with subtle launch and nitro cues; sound pauses with the run. The memorable moment is the foreground frame slowly turning through the fog as the reflective road and atmosphere accelerate.
