# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Potential employers evaluating Nishaj M for software-development roles, freelance clients looking for a developer for a project, and open-source or community collaborators assessing shared interests and capabilities.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Nishaj M as a full-stack software developer and designer. It helps visitors explore selected work, technical skills, and contact routes, with success measured by job opportunities, project enquiries, and open-source or community connections.

## Positioning

The portfolio combines a personal developer profile with project-level context, showing work across full-stack web development, automation, and interactive 3D experiments rather than only listing technologies.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the website on the web, review selected projects and skills, and can reach Nishaj through GitHub, LinkedIn, X, or email. The primary site includes home, projects, individual project-detail, and skills routes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built as a Next.js web application with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Three.js-related dependencies.
- Current primary content includes selected projects, a skills list, and direct social/contact links.
- The site should support professional evaluation, client enquiries, and community connection without inventing qualifications, client relationships, or project outcomes.
- No additional non-negotiable product constraints were specified during initialization.

## Brand Commitments

- Identity: Nishaj M (also known as Muhammed Nishaj M and nishaj0).
- Role: full-stack software developer and designer.
- Existing public contact routes: GitHub, LinkedIn, X, and email.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project summaries and technology tags are defined in `src/data/projects.ts`.
- Skills and portfolio copy are in `src/components/`.
- The public project image asset is `public/project-stock.jpg`.
- Some project links and media URLs use placeholder/example destinations; future work must not represent them as verified live demonstrations.

## Product Principles

1. Let real work and demonstrated capabilities make the case.
2. Make it easy for evaluators to understand both breadth and technical focus.
3. Give employers, clients, and collaborators a direct path to connect.
4. Preserve accuracy in project claims, links, and professional identity.
